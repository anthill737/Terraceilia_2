# Everything broken, missing or not working, and the plan (2026-11-10)

How this list was made: a 60-day run with every village's event log saved and counted (what
actually happens in play), a line-by-line wiring audit of the code (55 defects), the same build
run at different game speeds, and a look at how SAELIG and other sims handle the same problems.
Each item says FIXED (in this pass, with a test), PARTLY, or OPEN.

## A. The economy as a whole

1. FIXED. **The game only worked at 10x on a slow frame.** Ticks came from a wall-clock timer;
   walking came from physics. In tests people got 2.67 seconds of walking per tick, in play at
   1x to 3x they got 1, could not finish their rounds, and the town starved. Same build: 9 people
   at 10x, dead at 3x. Ticks now follow the physics clock (docs/WORKING_TOWN.md).
2. FIXED. **Newcomers with no work.** Anyone came when food looked fine, found nothing to do,
   went broke and left (about 25 per village in 120 days). Now a newcomer comes only while at
   most one person in town is without work.
3. FIXED. **The jobless went broke and left.** They now live as cottagers (garden plot, gathered
   wood, no rent asked) until someone hires them.
4. FIXED. **Profit piled up and went nowhere.** People's coins tripled in 120 days and sat with
   three owners. Owners now put spare money into improving their homes (logs and hired builders).
5. PARTLY. **Not enough paid work.** One baker and one woodworker supplied the whole town alone.
   Their output alone is cut (15 to 10, 12 to 10) so a growing town needs hands, and building
   and improving give builders work. Still open: with only bread and firewood to buy, a town
   settles at about 10 people. Real growth needs more things to make and buy (section C).
6. FIXED. **No trade between villages in normal play.** A travelling merchant per village now
   carries goods when one market pays more than the other charges. Trade is on by default.
7. FIXED. **No troubles, so balance was never tested.** Blight, bakery fire, hard frost and rats
   now happen, and TestShocks checks prices rise, fall back, and nobody starves.
8. FIXED. **Hired hands were invisible.** A hand now walks to the field, bakery or yard, and the
   inspector says who they work for.
9. PARTLY. **Nobody buys a house.** A renter who has saved the price now buys the town house they
   live in, and the feature test proves it. In normal play it stays rare for the first months,
   because a hand saves about a coin a day.
10. OPEN. **Wheat sits at its 1.00 floor.** A farm worker brings in about 10 wheat a day at a wage
    of about 6, so wheat costs about 0.60 to grow. The floor is above the cost, so farmers always
    overproduce and the store sits full. To lift it off the floor, farm output per worker has to
    fall (a first try at that broke the farmer's field rounds and was backed out) or the floor
    has to go. Blight does lift it (TestShocks).
11. FIXED. **Firewood ran out** on 5 to 14% of days. Builders only take surplus wood, cottagers
    gather their own, and the last runs show at most one cold day per village in 60.
12. FIXED. **New businesses opened into full markets and starved; broke owners starved instead of
    closing** (PR 17; still holding: no starvation deaths in any run of this pass).

## B. Wiring defects from the code audit

Fixed in this pass:
- Prosperity counted a well-fed town as hungry (hunger term upside down) and was recalculated
  and logged 10 times a day.
- Newcomers, the first woodworker and every household-opened business had no link to their own
  village: the inspector could not show their home, and they could never trade.
- An empty village froze for good with no message. It now keeps running, can be resettled, and a
  banner says it died out.
- Over the top stock band with stock still rising, the price got no downward push (wrong sign).
- "Better by a margin" passed worse options when the current score was negative.
- A failed owner sitting at exactly 0 coins was handed 60 new coins.
- Housing: arrivals before the houses were handed out on day 1; a house with no wood or worker
  stalled forever and blocked all building (given up after 20 days now); a homeless builder did
  not move into the house they finished; an owner's unpaid rent piled up and evicted them the
  day they closed shop; rent, wages and house prices were missing from the day's books, which
  also made owners let hands go while building; work marks stayed on people who changed trade;
  the inspector crashed on a landlord who had died.
- Old leave rules: failing to top up a bread reserve counted as a hungry day and drove people out.
- HUD showed what the market pays producers as the wheat and bread price (firewood showed the
  shelf price). World and toolbar counts left out woodworkers. FARMER and BAKER panels followed a
  person who was no longer one; there was no woodworker panel.
- Inspector: stale rules text (14 days and $200, real rules 7 and $100), a STARVING tag that could
  never show, two lines that could never show, Village 1's numbers shown for Village 2 people,
  no word on who a hand works for.
- Wealth colours used $1000 and $4000 when purses run $60 to a few hundred: everyone was "poor".
- Trade: off unless you pressed the button; the panel's profit section read a value nothing set;
  the panel never hid. Trade timing was in the wrong units (PR 17).
- Log panel rewrote 200 lines for every message and never trimmed its buffer.
- Toolbar placement always spawned into the selected village, wherever you clicked.
- The baker's log line said wheat when it meant bread (PR 17).

Still open (small, none blocks play):
- The old "career" evaluation still runs every 7 days for everyone and logs CAREER lines in the
  old switching language. It is how a household decides to open a business, so it stays; the
  wording and the inspector's career tab (which knows only farmer and baker) need a rewrite.
- A role's "average income" is the sum for the whole trade, not per person, so a second farmer
  looks twice as good as it is. The no-demand gate now stops the harm.
- config/economy_config.json: growth threshold 0.70 and cooldown 3 days override the code's 0.60
  and 1; `pause_threshold`, `prosperity.enabled` and the `population_growth` block are never read;
  farming's scarcity weight is 20 against baking's 2.
- The market's "buy blocked" flags are never set during play (only producers' own trade trips
  read them, and those are now off outside one test).
- Farmers and bakers still live at the farmhouse and bakery spots, not in the house with their
  name on it.
- Fields left by a farmer who is gone stay unowned until a new farm takes one.
- Unpaid upkeep is still written in the books as paid.
- Market decay uses the global random source, so runs are not exactly repeatable once it starts.
- Dead code: the admin menu and placement controller scenes, a few unused signals and helpers.

## C. Things you asked for that are not built

- More trades: iron mine and miners, blacksmith (nails, tools for farmers, swords), flax, linen,
  tailor. This is the real answer to item A5.
- Taxes and town spending: land tax, market tax, roads and other works paid from the town purse.
  (SAELIG: land tax per plot, market tax, market profits pay watchmen and other town jobs.)
- Families: one person per household today, so nobody's family helps build.
- Seasons.
- People moving to the other village instead of leaving the map (SAELIG does this).

## D. What other games do, and what was taken from it

- SAELIG: free houses gate arrivals (kept); the poor get by on a free bunkhouse (cottagers here);
  rent follows house value (kept); AI landlords build to rent, only when the market has spare
  wood (kept); travelling merchants move goods between town markets that each price from their
  own stock (the merchant); the developer's fix for worker shortages was to make the AI weigh free
  workers before starting businesses (arrivals follow work here). SAELIG still reports worker
  shortages and buy-and-sell-back exploits in 2025: it has eased these problems, not ended them.
- Victoria 3: the jobless work subsistence plots; owners put a share of profit into new building;
  harvest conditions as shocks.
- BazaarBot: a newcomer takes the trade in shortest supply (the no-demand gate from PR 17).
- Manor Lords, Songs of Syx, Dwarf Fortress: arrivals follow approval or wealth, plus a free house.
- X4, Patrician: each market prices from its own stock and traders chase the gap.
